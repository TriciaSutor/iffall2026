Jalapeño Popper Dip

This is a spicy and creamy dip that's perfect for parties! I have been using the recipe for years so it's an adaptation of the original version to make it more delicious!

Dietary: Gulten-Free, Vegetarian

Common Allergies: Dairy

Total Time: 25 mins; Prep time: 5 minutes

Yields 10-12 Servings

Ingredients

  • 16 oz chive-flavored whipped cream cheese, softened
  • 16 oz sour cream
  • 2 cup shredded cheddar cheese (extra for topping)
  • 4 diced jalapeños
  • 1 ranch seasoning packet

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine cream cheese, cheddar cheese, jalapeños, ranch seasoning, and sour cream.
  3. Transfer the mixture to a baking dish and spread it evenly. Add extra cheddar cheese on top.
  4. Bake for 20 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown.
  5. Broil the dip for 2-3 minutes to get a nice golden top. Watch it carefully to not burn.
  6. Serve warm with tortilla chips or sliced vegetables.
